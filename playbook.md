YouTube Content Strategy for B2B SaaS
Playbook

Overview

This playbook is based on my research of ten YouTube creators who regularly share practical advice about B2B SaaS growth, content marketing, startup strategy, and AI.

Instead of copying one creator's approach, I compared ideas from different experts, looked for common patterns, and selected the strategies that are practical for a small or growing B2B SaaS company.

The goal of this playbook is to provide a simple step-by-step process that can help founders and marketing teams plan, create, publish, and improve YouTube content that builds trust and supports business growth.

Every recommendation in this playbook is based on the research collected in this repository. Wherever possible, I have included the source of the recommendation so it is clear which expert influenced that idea.

Goal

The main goal of this playbook is to help a B2B SaaS company build a YouTube channel that:

* Attracts the right audience
* Builds trust through educational content
* Shares real business experience
* Generates qualified leads
* Supports long-term business growth

Rather than focusing on quick viral success, this playbook focuses on creating valuable content consistently and improving over time.
